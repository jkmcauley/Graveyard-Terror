This is the "Zombie Pack - HD PBR Rigged Characters" asset, containing 3D models of a rigged zombie characters. All models feature an LOD system.

Textures are located in the Textures folder.

Models are stored in the Models folder.

Materials can be found in the Materials folder.

Prefabs are placed in the Prefabs folder.

For your convenience, a demo scene is included.

Texture resolution: 4096x4096.
Supported render pipelines: Both URP and HDRP textures are provided.
